01 — TAKEAWAYS

Three things to keep.

  1. Brand ≠ logo — it's a complete system. The logo is just the signature.
  2. Useful beats beautiful — design for the user, not your portfolio.
  3. Research + systems are your moat — single designs are cheap, thinking is not.
