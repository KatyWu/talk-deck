02 — WORKING ENGLISH

Clear > Fluent.

Native speakers value precision over pretty sentences. If your idea is clear, you've passed the bar.

02 — WORKING ENGLISH

Three tips.

/ Tip 01

Everyone has their own way of speaking

Everyone has their own go-to phrases and words — observe and borrow them.

/ Tip 02

Use AI to polish, not write

Draft it yourself first. Then clean it up.

/ Tip 03

Have a small talk answer ready

Two sentences. Recent and real.

02 — WORKING LIFE
Foreign Company

The good.

02 — THE GOOD
/ Good 01

Way less hierarchy.

You can push back on your manager — and you're usually encouraged to. Frame it as a suggestion. Show your reasoning.

02 — THE GOOD
/ Good 02

Work-life balance is real.

Work hard when you're on. When you're off — actually be off. I once logged on at 2am and got called out for it.

02 — THE GOOD
/ Good 03

They treat you like an adult.

They respect your work and your time. In return: show up, do the work, own your mistakes.

02 — WORKING LIFE
Foreign Company

The hard.

02 — THE HARD
/ Hard 01

They are very polite.

"Sounds interesting." = please change this. My MA professor said "great work" every meeting — then gave me 60%.

02 — THE HARD
/ Hard 02

Self-discipline is your ticket.

Nobody checks in. Log what you did. Sync regularly. When you have a problem — bring a solution, not just a question.

02 — THE HARD
/ Hard 03

Things move slower.

Buffer every deadline. "I'll get back to you soon" does not mean today. Unexpected things happen more than you expect.

03 — IMAGE

Images — before vs after.

Before AI

  • Hunt for reference images
  • Photoshop collage / manual edit
  • Hours to get a rough concept

After AI

  • Write prompt → generate → refine
  • Minutes to usable concept image
  • Photoshop / Figma for finishing
03 — IMAGE

My image process.

  1. Feed reference to Claude → get a precise prompt
  2. Drop prompt into Freepik (with reference image)
  3. Finish in Photoshop / Figma — add type, fix details
03 — IMAGE

Freepik

03 — IMAGE

Image tools.

/ Use

Freepik · Midjourney

Fast first drafts. Use nano-banana or GPT-image-2 models.

/ Know this

Generated = not editable

AI output is a starting point. Always finish in Photoshop or Figma — you can't edit the pixels directly.

03 — VIDEO

Video — before vs after.

Before AI

↗ Image Bank
  • Shoot footage or find stock
  • Manual edit in Premiere / Final Cut
  • Days for even a short clip

After AI

  • Node-based generation per segment
  • Stitch outputs together
  • Hours, not days
03 — VIDEO

Freepik Spaces —
node canvas.

  1. Storyboard first — one node per scene
  2. Each node output feeds the next node
  3. Export segment by segment, assemble in editor

03 — VIDEO

Video tools.

/ Model

Kling 3.0

Consistent motion, follows prompts well. Struggles with complex shots and long clips.

Freepik Spaces

Node-based canvas — connect outputs into a sequence and export as video.

Premiere · or any browser editor

order matters.

  1. Setup environment → header / footer

  3. UI done → wire up functions
  4. Visual confirmed → add animation + polish

04 — CONCLUSION

Skills that compound.

01 Research — find the right problem first
02 Systems thinking — deliver rules, not single files
03 Writing & presenting — sell what you make
04 Communication — sell what you make
05 AI fluency — not replaced, accelerated
04 — CONCLUSION
/ Ch.01

Pick skills that compound.

Ch.01 — A designer's real value isn't in the files they deliver. It's in the systems and thinking behind them. Learn that, and the tools don't matter.

04 — CONCLUSION
/ Ch.02

Learn to write & communicate.

Ch.02 — Foreign teams run on text. If you can write clearly, you can work with anyone, anywhere. Clear thinking is the only kind that travels across time zones.

04 — CONCLUSION
/ Ch.03

Build in public.

Ch.03 — AI gives you speed. But what you do with that time — sharing your process, building in the open — that's what makes you visible and findable.

04 — CONCLUSION

Stay curious.

About everything. People, tools, ideas, other industries. The designers who keep growing are the ones who never stop asking why.
